My goals for this course are to learn and develop skills in HTML and CSS as well as learn how to create professional and accessible websites. A bonus would be to end up assembling an electronic portfolio that highlights my progress. I feel that these goals closely connect with the academic learning outcomes of this course which include creating webpages using HTML and CSS, following web accessibility and WCAG guidelines, using web development frameworks and also maintaining an e-portfolio of school and design work. Research on student portfolios shows that portfolios can help students document their performance and demonstrate their development of competencies (Oudkerk Pool et al., 2020). Similarly, Chang et al. (2018) found that e-portfolios can support students in setting learning goals and becoming more involved in their own learning. My personal goals are quite similar to the academic learning outcomes because they both focus on developing as well as demonstrating web design skills. I would only say that my goals are more focused on becoming very confident in the utilisation of said skills and also building the portfolio soI can show my work in the not so distant future.
